Catch abnormal diesel use early

Diesel used above the approved norm by dumpers, shovels and other machines is often noticed too late. This tool shows those exceptions the moment they are recorded, so the Fuel Manager and E&M Manager can act sooner.

In a few seconds the dashboard answers:

  1. How many diesel exceptions are there, and is it getting better or worse?
  2. How much diesel was used, and what did it cost?
  3. Which mines and locations have the most exceptions?
  4. Which vehicles and equipment types use the most fuel?
  5. How is fuel efficiency (km per litre) changing?
  6. Which exceptions are still open, and what should we do now?
  7. Live alerts: is any vehicle above or below its expected diesel right now?

The suggestions are decision support based on recorded exceptions. They are not accusations of fuel misuse.
